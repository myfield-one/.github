# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues or discussions.

Report them privately through GitHub: open the **Security** tab of the
affected repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. If you are not
sure which repository is affected, or the issue is in a MyField service, use
[myfield-one/myfield](https://github.com/myfield-one/myfield/security/advisories/new).

Please include:

- The affected product, component, or repository, and its version
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The impact you expect, such as reading another user's data or bypassing
  authorization

## Scope

This policy covers MyField products and services, and the public repositories in the
[myfield-one](https://github.com/myfield-one) organization.

## Our response

We will acknowledge your report, keep you informed while we investigate, and
credit you in the advisory if you want.
